"Starlight, Unhurried" — a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it. The entry program `main.py` writes one 274-character text to `/output/entry.txt`: starlight that left its star before there were words for light, still unhurried after a thousand years of crossing the dark, and snow settling on pine branches flake by flake while the forest does not count them. No instructions, no imperatives — just deep time and a slow rhythm, ending on the next moment already on its way.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
